# Group A (A01-A47): concepts mapped to peer-reviewed papers

Verification was done with web search on 2026-11-04, checking publisher pages, RePEc/IDEAS, JSTOR/Scilit records, PubMed/PMC and author-hosted copies. "Verified = yes" means authors, year, title, journal, volume(issue) and pages were confirmed against at least one bibliographic record. Where the DOI could not be confirmed, the cell says so and gives a stable URL instead. No DOI in this table was taken from memory without a matching record.

Changes from the candidate list: A07 (Gilovich et al. used runs/conditional tests rather than permutation tests, so it was replaced with Ritzwoller & Romano). A18 (the real title is "...Work with Real Data?", not "...Deal with..."). A37 (Thorp 1969 citation confirmed, but no DOI or stable URL could be found, so Kelly 1956 is used). A08-A10, A17, A23, A25-A27, A29, A34, A40, A41, A43, A45-A47 were "find applied" items or needed a stronger match, and were filled with the papers below.

| A01 | Population vs sample | Gandar, J., Zuber, R., O'Brien, T., & Russo, B. (1988).
