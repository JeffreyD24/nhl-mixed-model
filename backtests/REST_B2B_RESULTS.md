## 2026-11-05: Rested team vs back-to-back team, pre-registered test on 2011-16 (NHL) [TEST] [CORRELATION]
- Question: does the rested team win more often than the no-vig CLOSING moneyline says when the opponent is on a back-to-back (catalog X02)? Seen in 2016-22 and 2024-26; it came from a ~98-check catalog, so it needed a clean test on older seasons (RULE 2).
- Data / method: `derived/oos_games.csv.gz` (SBR lines + hockeyR schedule), END-year seasons 2012-2016 (2011-12 .. 2015-16), trusted decided games with exactly one team on a b2b = 1,424 games. Rules fixed in `backtests/REST_B2B_PREREG.md` (SHA-256 6bdfa1a7...e4253, saved to the project at 2026-11-05 22:49 UTC before the run; could not be pushed to GitHub from this session). Script `dev/rest_b2b_test.py`, output `dev/rest_b2b_test_output.txt`. b2b definition validated first on 2017-22 (reproduced 1,520 games, +33.6u, per-season ROIs).
- Result: **PASS.** Rested team won 58.1% vs 55.4% no-vig close (53.9% at the open). Primary win - close = +2.7 pts (SE 1.3, 90% CI +0.6 to +4.8, one-sided p = 0.017), positive in 4 of 5 seasons (2012 +7.4, 2013 +1.9, 2014 -2.3, 2015 +4.1, 2016 +2.4). Price moved toward the rested team in 5 of 5 seasons (+1.45 pts on average). Secondary: ROI at the opening price +3.7% (+53.1u). Home-rested +2.9 pts (n=1,087), away-rested +2.2 pts (n=337). Pooled 2011-22 (descriptive): 2,944 games, +2.5 pts (95% CI +0.8 to +4.3), 8 of 11 seasons positive, ROI at the open +2.9% (95% CI -0.4% to +6.2%).
- Read: third independent period with the same gap (2011-16 +2.7, 2016-22 +2.3, 2024-26 +3.9), so the market's price for a back-to-back, even at the close, looks too low by about 2-3 points. This is a correlation: a back-to-back team's backup-goalie starts and fatigue are plausible mechanisms but were not separated here. Strength: pre-registered, single primary test, out of sample; but one test with power about 57%, and the profit at the open (+2.9% pooled) is still not statistically separable from zero after the vig. The 2012-13 lockout season (n=180) is included.
- Decision: nothing changes in live rules or alerts (no approval requested). The live `state/rest_b2b_test.csv` TEST log keeps running. Suggested follow-ups (not run): split by whether the b2b team's goalie is a backup (RULE 2 data: hockeyR goalies in `oos_games_g.csv.gz`); check whether the model already prices it (catalog A03 says the shot model mostly does) so this is not double-counted as a filter.
